# Security Policy · Politica de Seguranca

## English

**Supported version.** The latest release on [nasmac.app](https://nasmac.app).

**Reporting.** Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Use
GitHub's [private advisory form](https://github.com/vprotti/usetokens/security/advisories/new),
which reaches me directly. Include what the flaw is, how to trigger it and what
an attacker gets out of it.

I will confirm receipt within a few days and keep you posted until it is fixed.
Credit in the release notes if you want it.

**Scope worth knowing about.** UseTokens runs unsandboxed, signed ad-hoc, and
installs no privileged helper or daemon. It has no server and no account
system. Reports about the missing Apple notarization are already known — it
requires the paid developer program.

## Portugues

**Versao suportada.** A ultima publicada no [nasmac.app](https://nasmac.app).

**Como reportar.** Por favor nao abra issue publica para falha de seguranca.
Use o [formulario privado do GitHub](https://github.com/vprotti/usetokens/security/advisories/new),
que chega direto em mim. Diga qual e a falha, como reproduzir e o que um
atacante consegue com ela.

Confirmo o recebimento em alguns dias e te mantenho informado ate a correcao.
Credito nas notas da versao, se voce quiser.

**Contexto util.** O UseTokens roda sem sandbox, assinado ad-hoc, e nao instala
helper privilegiado nem daemon. Nao tem servidor nem sistema de conta. Relatos
sobre a falta de notarizacao da Apple ja sao conhecidos — ela depende do
programa pago de desenvolvedor.
