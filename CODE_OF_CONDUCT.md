# Code of Conduct · Codigo de Conduta

## English

Be decent. That is the whole thing, but to be concrete:

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks. No sexual content.
- Do not post anyone's private information.
- Off-topic arguing gets closed, not debated.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the
project shows up.

Something out of line? Report it privately through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or open a
private security advisory on this repository. Reports are handled quietly. I
maintain this project alone, so I am the one who reads them, and I may edit,
delete or block as needed.

## Portugues

Seja decente. E basicamente isso, mas para ficar concreto:

- Presuma que a outra pessoa esta agindo de boa-fe.
- Critique o codigo, nao quem escreveu.
- Nada de assedio, xingamento ou ataque pessoal. Nada de conteudo sexual.
- Nao publique informacao privada de ninguem.
- Discussao fora do assunto e fechada, nao debatida.

Vale para issues, pull requests, discussoes e qualquer lugar onde o projeto
aparecer.

Viu algo fora da linha? Reporte em particular pelo
[formulario do GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) ou abra um
aviso de seguranca privado neste repositorio. Os relatos sao tratados sem
alarde. Eu mantenho o projeto sozinho, entao sou eu que leio, e posso editar,
apagar ou bloquear conforme necessario.
